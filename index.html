Calculation Settings

These settings apply to new payments only. Existing transactions keep the exact formula values used when they were saved.

FORMULA PREVIEW
Net weight = actual weight − weight deduction; amount = net weight × price; labour = actual weight × labour rate; final deduction = amount after labour × percentage.

Defaults: 3.5 kg, ₹25, and 1%. Values must be valid and non-negative. Weight deduction cannot exceed 100 kg per quintal; percentage cannot exceed 100%.
